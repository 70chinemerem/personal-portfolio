### 2. Analytics & Tracking (RECOMMENDED)
- ❌ No Google Analytics
- ❌ No tracking pixels
- ❌ No conversion tracking

### 3. Contact Information (CRITICAL)
- ❌ Placeholder email: "your.email@example.com"
- ❌ Placeholder social links (href="#")
- ❌ Need real contact details

### 4. Form Functionality (IMPORTANT)
- ❌ Contact form doesn't actually send emails
- ❌ Need backend integration or form service (Formspree, Netlify Forms, etc.)

### 5. Error Handling (IMPORTANT)
- ❌ No 404 page
- ❌ No error boundaries
- ❌ Missing robots.txt
- ❌ Missing sitemap.xml

### 6. Security (RECOMMENDED)
- ❌ No Content Security Policy
- ❌ No security headers
- ❌ Form needs CSRF protection (if backend)

### 7. Accessibility (IMPORTANT)
- ⚠️ Need to verify ARIA labels
- ⚠️ Need keyboard navigation testing
- ⚠️ Need color contrast verification
