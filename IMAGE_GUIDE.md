## Image Tips

1. **Quality**: Use high-resolution images (minimum 1920px width for hero, 800px+ for others)
2. **Format**: Use WebP or compressed JPG for better performance
3. **Style**: Maintain consistent style - bright, professional, modern
4. **Lighting**: Ensure good lighting and composition
5. **Relevance**: Use images that match the section content
6. **Optimization**: Compress images before adding (use tools like TinyPNG or ImageOptim)
